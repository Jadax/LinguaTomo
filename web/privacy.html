LinguaTomo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026 · Applies to LinguaTomo on Web, Android and iOS

In short: LinguaTomo is local-first. Your learning progress lives on your device by default. Creating an account is optional and only adds backup and cross-device sync. There are no advertisements, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and nothing is ever sold.

What stays on your device

Without an account, everything LinguaTomo knows about you stays on your device, stored locally (via Hive). This includes:

  • Vocabulary, kana, kanji and grammar progress, streaks and XP
  • Achievement, postcard and Nest collection state
  • Handwriting practice scores
  • App preferences (experience mode, sound, level)

Photos you capture for handwriting grading are processed for on-device text recognition (Google ML Kit) and are not uploaded anywhere by LinguaTomo.

If you create an account

Signing in is entirely optional and uses a passwordless email link. If you sign in:

  • Your email address is held by our authentication provider, Supabase, solely to send you sign-in links and identify your account.
  • Your learning progress snapshot (the same data listed above) is copied to a Supabase database so it can sync across your devices.
  • If you choose to join the optional achievement board, your chosen nickname, achievement count and XP become visible to other learners who also joined. Your email address is never shown to anyone.

Supabase acts strictly as our infrastructure provider (a data processor) for this optional feature — we do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not use your data for advertising.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad networks or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No selling or renting of personal data
  • No public community features, direct messaging or user-generated content today

Children

LinguaTomo's content is family-friendly and suitable for learners of all ages, but the app is not directed primarily at children and account sign-in is not designed for use by children without a parent or guardian's involvement. Core learning always works fully without creating an account.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything stored in your account at any time from Account & Sync → Delete my cloud data in the app. This immediately deletes your synced progress and profile from our database and signs you out; your local, on-device learning is untouched and keeps working offline.

To also delete your sign-in identity (your email record) entirely, email us at astraiva.apps@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and we will remove it, typically within a few days.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to astraiva.apps@gmail.com.
